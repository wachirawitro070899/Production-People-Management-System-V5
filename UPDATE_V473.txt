V473 - Cross Device Live Sync

Changes:
- Prevent Firebase realtime updates from being discarded while this device is writing.
- Queue the newest remote snapshot and apply it immediately after the write finishes.
- Pull fresh Firebase data every 15 seconds while the page is visible.
- Refresh from Firebase when the browser/tab gets focus or becomes visible again.
- Refresh immediately after reconnecting to the internet.
- Bump app.js and employees-data.js cache version to v=473.
- Add cache-control hints to index.html to reduce stale pages on other computers.

Expected result:
Attendance / organization chart changes written to Firebase should appear on other computers without requiring a hard refresh (normally realtime; 15-second polling is a fallback).
