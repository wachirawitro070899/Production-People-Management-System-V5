V478 - Cloud Shift Authoritative

- Updated from V477.
- Shift schedules are now treated as Firebase-authoritative after migration.
- Existing V477 local shift schedules are migrated/merged to Firebase during the V478 migration path.

- Day/Night shift separation in Attendance and Organization Chart continues to use the shared cloud shift schedule.
