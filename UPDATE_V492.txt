V492 - Day Shift rollover at 19:00

1. At 19:00 or later, employees assigned to Day Shift no longer keep the current-day live badge.
2. Their badge changes to "รอเช็คชื่อวันถัดไป" and shows the next date/check-in time.
3. If the next day is a holiday (including automatic Sunday), the badge shows "วันถัดไป • วันหยุด" and the holiday name.
4. If the employee is scheduled to switch to Night Shift on the next day, the badge shows the next Night Shift check-in instead.
5. Cache query updated to v492.
6. V491 fixed Supervisor/Leader placement is preserved.
