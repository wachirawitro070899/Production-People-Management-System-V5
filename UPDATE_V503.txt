V503 - Attendance Cloud Verification Fix

- Prevent automatic absence reconciliation until Attendance Firebase master is fully loaded.
- Existing local check-in is now verified against central Attendance records when employee taps Check-in again.
- If the phone has a valid local check-in but the central record is missing, the system automatically re-uploads/repairs it.
- If central verification cannot be completed, the local record is marked pending so the employee can retry without losing the original timestamp.
- Admin-deleted attendance dates remain protected and are not resurrected.
