V534 Attendance retro leave rules
- Sick retroactive leave: only within 3 days, medical certificate required, -3.
- Sick retroactive over 3 days: rejected; remains absence -10.
- Personal retroactive leave: only within 3 days, -4; same-day after cutoff -4.
- Personal retroactive over 3 days: rejected; remains absence -10.
- Auto absence remains visible until a valid leave notice arrives within 3 days; then the same employee/date record is replaced with leave and KPI deduction recalculates to the leave rule.
- Medical-certificate late attachment window reduced to 3 days.
