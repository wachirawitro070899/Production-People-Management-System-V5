V543 - Attendance re-check after Admin permanent delete

- Admin permanent delete still creates a tombstone to block stale cached Attendance from returning.
- A genuinely new check-in created AFTER the Admin deletion is now allowed.

- Old cached check-in timestamps from before the deletion remain blocked.
- Updated app.js cache version in index.html and 404.html to v=543.
