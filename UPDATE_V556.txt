V556 - Examination History hard lock

- When an exam is auto-submitted because the employee leaves/switches tabs, the exam page immediately re-renders to the submitted receipt.
- Any existing exam history record locks the matching exam so the form is no longer displayed.
- Receipt clearly states that the exam has been submitted and cannot be taken again.
- Forced-exit receipts show answered/total count.
- Permanent-placement exam keeps the 1 attempt per calendar month rule.
